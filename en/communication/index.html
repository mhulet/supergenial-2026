Digital communication

Telling the story of transition, without greenwashing.

You are changing things, but your website, newsletter and social channels don't show it yet. We write and build your communication with the same standard Semisto holds itself to: facts before adjectives, dated figures, and light tools that actually serve.

Honest communication proves. Greenwashing promises.

Your readers can tell the difference. We keep the proof up front, and the enthusiasm right behind it.

What we do

Four kinds of work, as fixed packages

Every package starts from a written scope and a price agreed before work begins. No hourly meter.

  • A lean, well-referenced website: fast, light, readable by search engines and AI assistants, and easy for your team to update
  • A newsletter: editorial line, template and a regular send
  • Social media: what to post, where, and at a pace you can keep up
  • A content strategy: what you have to say, to whom, and in what order
Who it's for

Organisations that carry sustainability

B Corp companies, NGOs, cooperatives, local producers: organisations with real results to show and no wish to overstate them.

The link with the mission

Your invoice grows forests

Super Génial is the digital service of Semisto, the association working to turn Europe into a network of edible forests. 20% of every invoice funds that work. The price doesn't change: the contribution is included.
