What is a "point"? Instead of hours, I use complexity points as the unit of value. Your subscription includes a monthly quota of points; beyond that, we switch to a feature package. You pay for the value delivered, not the time it took me.
  • 1 pt Small tweak — a field, a label, a color
  • 2 pts Small improvement — a filter, a sort, an automatic email
  • ~5 pts Standard feature — a full screen

Good to know

FAQ

Why not hourly billing?
Because with AI, the same feature can take ten times less time than before. Billing by the hour would reward slowness and penalize speed — the opposite of what serves you. You pay for a result at a price known in advance.
Are unused points carried over?
The quota renews each month and does not roll over. If your needs grow lastingly, we adjust the tier rather than accumulating.
Can I change tier or cancel?
Yes. The subscription is commitment-free, changeable or cancellable from one month to the next. The goal is for it to serve you, not to trap you.
Is the subscription mandatory after launch?
No, but strongly recommended: an application in production needs to be monitored and maintained. Without a subscription, work is done as feature packages, case by case.
