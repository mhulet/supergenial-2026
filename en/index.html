The spreadsheets that run the company

Orders, registrations, stock, schedules: everything goes through a file everyone is afraid to open. It costs nothing to buy. You pay for it in hours of copying, in mistakes and in depending on a single person.

What is Semisto?

Turning Europe into a network of edible forests.

That is the mission of Semisto, the association I founded. It designs, plants and teaches food forests: food-producing ecosystems that regenerate soil, biodiversity and the bonds between people. Super Génial is its IT service.

Every euro invoiced goes to Semisto ASBL, with no shareholders. It pays for my work and funds the mission. Your IT budget also becomes support you can cite in your impact report.
  • Design Labsthe design studio that draws food forests

  • Semisto Designerthe online tool to design your own food forest
  • Academytraining, from one day to thirty

  • Food Forest Heroesthe volunteers who plant, to music
