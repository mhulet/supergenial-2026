How it works

Two weeks, five steps

I come to you, listen to your team describe their actual working day, and leave with what I need to map the whole company. Your time: about one hour per person interviewed, plus the final walkthrough.

  1. 01 Kickoff: your priorities, your constraints, your current tools
  2. 02 Team interviews — the people who do the work, not just management
  3. 03 Mapping of processes and information flows
  4. 04 Analysis: every AI opportunity scored on impact (hours saved) and feasibility (data, tools, cost)
  5. 05 Walkthrough: the report presented and discussed with you, decisions in hand

Good to know

FAQ

Why pay, when free AI audits exist?
A free audit is a sales pitch in disguise: it always concludes you should buy whatever its author sells. Here you pay for real work — interviews, mapping, numbers — and the report is yours. You can act on it with me, with someone else, or on your own.
What if the assessment concludes there is nothing worth doing?
Then the report will say so, with the reasons. It's rare — there are almost always a few hours a week to win back — but knowing you should not invest right now saves you far more than the cost of an assessment.
Do we need to be digitized already for this to make sense?
No. We start from your real processes, even if they live on paper, in notebooks, or in two people's heads. That is often exactly where the assessment uncovers the most value.
How much of my team's time does it take?
About one hour per person interviewed, a kickoff meeting at the start, and a 90-minute walkthrough at the end. The rest of the work happens on my side.
